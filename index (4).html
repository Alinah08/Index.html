Course Details

The package for this makeup class includes comprehensive instruction in essential beauty techniques. You'll learn everything from proper skin preparation and base application to advanced skills like blending eyeshadow, defining brows, and mastering contouring and highlighting. The course also covers lash application and provides valuable client tips and product knowledge, making it a well-rounded experience for aspiring makeup artists or enthusiasts.

What this course includes:

This makeup course package is designed to give my students a comprehensive learning experience. Upon completion, each student will receive a certificate of completion to signify their mastery of the course material. Additionally, they'll get an exclusive eBook packed with all the techniques and knowledge covered in the class, serving as a valuable resource they can refer back to.

This course is for you if you:

  • want to learn professional makeup techniques
  • are interested in skincare and makeup application
  • aspire to become a makeup artist
  • want to improve your professional makeup skills
  • want to become your own boss!
